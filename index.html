      FAQ

      How fast is delivery?

      This is a demo, but the UI simulates a 10-minute express slot.

      Which coupons work?

      Try FIRST50, SAVE10 or FREEDEL.

      Is my data stored?

      Only in your own browser (localStorage). Nothing leaves your device.
